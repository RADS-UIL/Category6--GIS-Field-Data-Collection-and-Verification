Zones

Zone outlines are drawn around each zone's interviews, not taken from official boundaries. Faqeer Abad and Gulbahar overlap because their interviews are interleaved on the ground.

Some positions are spread for display. Households recorded at the same spot are shown spread along the nearest streets, and each zone's commercial units are shown in a line along the nearest main road. Click a point to see where it was actually recorded. The data files keep the recorded GPS.

Clusters (dashed outlines, lettered A, B, C) group interviews within about 250 m of each other inside a zone. The label shows the number of interviews.

A dashed outline on a household dot marks a location estimated during data cleaning.
